HT1
Design and Technology: Childs Toy Project. Develop 3D drawing techniques. Understand the properties and uses of different woods and metals. Learn the reasons behind modelling.

HT2
Childs Toy Project. Manufacture the toy using machine and hand tools. Understand forces. Electronics: understand circuits, resistance and a systems approach to design.

HT6
Food: Design and make pizza. Understand how yeast works. Create a healthy pasta bake. Understand where our food comes from.
